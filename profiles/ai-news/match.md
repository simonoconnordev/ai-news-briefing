# AI news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in artificial intelligence: model releases and benchmarks, AI lab announcements, AI products and developer tools, open-weight models, research results, AI safety and policy, AI industry moves, and community discussions about using or building with AI.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is a complete long-form argument, a step-by-step tutorial, a full academic paper, or promotional material without substantive information.
