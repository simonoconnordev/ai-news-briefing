# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news for a reader who wants a daily "what's new in AI" briefing: new models and capabilities, AI lab and product announcements, developer tools and agents, open-weight models, notable research, AI safety and policy, and significant AI industry news.

# Relevance rule

The item must be primarily about AI, machine learning, or LLMs. Items that are not about AI score at most 3, however important they are in general technology. Items that only mention AI in passing score at most 4.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Must know.** Major model or product launches from leading labs, significant capability jumps, landmark research, or industry-changing AI announcements.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable releases, strong open-weight models, important tools or agent frameworks, insightful analysis of a recent AI event, or significant safety and policy developments.
- **5-6: Worth a look.** Useful smaller releases, practical findings from people building with AI, interesting research, or community discussions with real insight.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, rumours without substance, shallow takes, or non-AI items.
- **0-2: Noise.** Memes, complaints without information, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider novelty, likely impact on people who use or build with AI, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Treat substantive community debate as additional evidence of value, but do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward hype, exaggerated headlines, or unsourced leaks.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
